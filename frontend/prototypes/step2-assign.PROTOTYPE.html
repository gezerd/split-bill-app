Step 2: assigning items with weighted Shares

Does the new assignment model feel right? Each Person pays item total × their Shares ÷ all Shares on the item. Tap an avatar to add or remove that person. Tap a card to open the Share sheet, where −/+ sets ×2 and up. Click a name in the people row to select that person, then tap cards to add them to items or take them off.
